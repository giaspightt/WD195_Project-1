About us

Coco Rico is a Puerto Rican soda produced since 1935 and has enjoyed acceptance across different ethnic communities.

Coco Rico is also available in diet version.

Coco Rico can be used as an ingredient in cooking. (natural coconut flavor)

Coco Rico has no artificial flavors or colorants.

Coco Rico has a unique and exotic soft coconut flavor very similar to fresh coconut water.

Coco Rico is an excellent mixer for cocktails and alcoholic drinks.

Coconut beverages are in trend and continued growth. Consumers are demanding for more products containing coconut and coconut oil (Coco Rico main Ingredient) because of the health benefits of the fruit.

Our products

Coco Rico is made of coconut and other natural ingredients and is currently produced in Glass Bottles, PET, Aluminum Cans and Tetra Pak. The Coco Rico Tetra Pak currently comes in 500ml and 1L. The Coco Rico PET currently comes in 16oz, 20oz and 2L. The Coco Rico Aluminum Can currently comes in 12oz and 25oz. The Coco Rico Glass Bottle currently comes in 7oz and 12oz.
We are constantly looking to innovate our products and packaging. Coconut Beverages are a very popular flavor profile and could be a great addition to your company’s profile.

The Coco Rico brand was introduced into super markets in Puerto Rico in 1935 and is currently available throughout the United States, Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Canada, Senegal, The Netherlands, Aruba and Curacao.
Coco Rico is always looking to expand our product line to different areas of the world, so if interested please contact us today

The Coco Rico Brand is family owned, so you will get all the attention of a family operated business.
